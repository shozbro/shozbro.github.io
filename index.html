about.spec.ts

Quality is a habit before it is a pipeline.

I started in QA as an intern at Coderatory in 2023, running manual test cases and learning to write bug reports that developers act on. From there I moved through Stixor Technologies, ASI and Mailmunch to Folio3, where I own quality for CXApp across iOS, Android and web.

Most of my work now sits where testing meets AI. I build Playwright suites with AI-assisted script generation, test LLM and agentic workflows, and help my team build tools that reproduce bugs and verify pull requests on their own.

I care about the boring parts too: clear reproduction steps, honest severity, test plans people read, and a release sign-off that means something.

Outside work I shoot photos, follow football and play games. The game at the bottom of this page is what happens when a QA engineer builds one.
